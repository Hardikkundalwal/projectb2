# Verification notes

Verified on 2026-10-07: the current official telc page still describes 3 reading parts plus 2 language-elements parts in a shared 90-minute block, 3 listening parts (~20 minutes), one writing task (30 minutes), and 3 speaking parts (~15 minutes, normally pairs, 20-minute preparation). The official 2021 mock supplies the 300-point weighting and separate 60% written/oral pass thresholds.

The product targets general telc Deutsch B2. Do not substitute specifications from B2+ Beruf, DTB B2, Medizin, or B2·C1 Beruf.
