# WRT-005 sample lesson — Halbformelle Anrede und Schlussformel

**Goal:** Choose an opening and closing that fit a message to a company, course provider, landlord, or institution. Estimated time: 8 minutes.

## Principle

Use respectful distance when the recipient is not a private friend. If a name is known, use “Sehr geehrte Frau …” or “Sehr geehrter Herr …”. If no person is named, use “Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren”. Close with “Mit freundlichen Grüßen”. Do not mix a formal opening with “Liebe Grüße”.

## Contrast

Private: “Hallo Mira, … Liebe Grüße”

Semi-formal: “Sehr geehrte Frau Baumann, … Mit freundlichen Grüßen”

## Example

You write to a course office whose contact person is Frau Neumann. A fitting frame is: “Sehr geehrte Frau Neumann, … Mit freundlichen Grüßen”.

## Micro-practice

Choose the best pair for an unknown employee at a travel company:

A. Hallo! / Bis bald
B. Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren / Mit freundlichen Grüßen
C. Liebe Leute / Viele Grüße

Answer: B. It keeps respectful distance and is internally consistent.

## Repair

If you chose A or C, label the relationship first: private, known professional, or unknown professional. Then choose both formulas from the same register.

## Recap

Recipient known? Use title + surname. Unknown? “Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren”. Close formally and consistently.
